# Seller Center guide

For businesses selling on the marketplace. The Seller Center is at `https://seller.<your-domain>`.

## 1. Register and get verified

1. _Register your business_: registered business name, business type (sole proprietorship,
   partnership, corporation, one-person corporation, cooperative), owner's name, business
   mobile number, e-mail and password.
2. **Confirm your e-mail** from the link we send.
3. _Business profile_: DTI/SEC/CDA registration number, BIR TIN, VAT registration, business
   address.
4. _Verification_: upload the **DTI/SEC/CDA certificate**, **BIR Certificate of Registration
   (Form 2303)** and the **owner's government-issued ID** (PDF, JPEG or PNG), then _Submit for
   verification_.
5. The marketplace reviews your application. You are notified when it is approved, or when
   more information is needed (the note says what; update and submit again).

Legal details and documents are locked while under review.

## 2. Open a store

_Stores › Create store_: store name (its web address is checked for availability), logo,
banner, description, contact details. A verified seller can run several stores.

## 3. Team

_Employees › Invite employee_: e-mail and role — **Manager**, **Encoder** (products),
**Inventory**, **Order processor**, **Accounting** (finance pages, read-only actions). The
employee accepts the invitation and works only on your business. Suspend or remove employees
at any time; their access ends immediately. Turn on two-step verification for your own account
(_Account settings_); we recommend it for everyone who can request payouts.

## 4. Warehouses and stock

- _Warehouses_: each location you ship from, with its exact barangay (shipping fees and
  delivery estimates are calculated from it). Mark the default.
- _Stock adjustments_: record stock in, adjustments, damaged units and transfers between
  warehouses. _Movement history_ keeps every change. Low-stock thresholds notify you.
- Stock is reserved when a customer places an order and released if the order is cancelled
  or not paid in time, so you never sell more than you have.

## 5. Products

1. _Products › Add product_: name, category, description, SKU, regular price (VAT-inclusive
   if you are VAT-registered), optional sale price and wholesale tiers.
2. Fill the category's **details** (e.g. net weight), add **photos** (at least one; JPEG, PNG,
   WebP), weight and size for shipping, and **variants** (sizes, colours) if needed.
3. _Submit for approval_. The marketplace approves it or tells you what to fix. Changes to an
   approved product's content go back to review; price and stock changes do not.

## 6. Orders

1. New orders appear in _Orders_ (tabs: New, To ship, Shipped, Completed, Cancelled, Returns).
   Only your part of a customer's order is visible to you.
2. **Accept and start preparing**, then **Book shipment** (or enter the courier and tracking
   number if you arrange delivery yourself). Print the waybill.
3. Cash on delivery: the courier collects; the amount counts as received when the courier's
   remittance is recorded by the marketplace.
4. Cancellation requests from customers: approve or refuse with a reason.

## 7. Returns

Customers can ask for a return within the return window with photos. **Approve** (refund or
replacement, with or without the item coming back) or **Refuse** with a reason. If the customer
escalates a refusal, the marketplace decides. Record the item when it comes back.

## 8. Promotions and engagement

- _Coupons_: your own codes (percentage, fixed amount, free shipping), with limits and dates.
- _Flash sales_ and _Campaigns_: time-limited prices, bundles, buy-one-take-one. The server
  applies the best deal per item automatically.
- _Reviews and ratings_: reply to reviews (one public reply each); report abusive ones.
- _Customer questions_: support tickets about your orders; reply within a day.

## 9. Money

| Page                   | What it shows                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overview               | Pending balance (orders still in their return window), available balance, what can be withdrawn now, this month's sales and deductions.                  |
| Transactions           | Every line that changed your balance: sales, commission, fees, refunds, adjustments.                                                                     |
| Settlements            | Each settlement period's statement: orders settled, gross sales, commission, payment fees, refunds, withholding tax, net amount; downloadable documents. |
| Payouts                | Request a payout of your available balance to a verified bank account; track approval and payment (with the transfer reference).                         |
| Bank accounts          | Add your business bank account; the marketplace verifies it before the first payout.                                                                     |
| Invoices / Tax profile | The marketplace's commission invoices to you; your BIR registration details (verified before the first payout).                                          |

How it works: when an order is delivered, its earnings are **pending** during the return
window. After it ends, the next scheduled **settlement** moves them to **available**, minus
commission, fees and any withholding tax. You can then request a **payout** (minimum amount in
the payout page). A refund after settlement is deducted from your available balance.

## 10. Reports

_Reports_: sales, orders, products, refunds, ratings and settlements for any period, by day,
week, month or year; export to Excel, CSV or PDF.

## 11. Good practice

- Keep stock accurate; overselling is prevented, but cancelling accepted orders hurts your rating.
- Ship on time and enter real tracking numbers.
- Never ask customers to pay outside the marketplace; payments outside are not protected.
