# Shopping guide

For customers of the marketplace at `https://shop.<your-domain>`.

## 1. Your account

- **Create account**: name, mobile number, e-mail and password. We text a 6-digit code to
  confirm your number. Confirm your e-mail from the link we send (needed for receipts and
  password resets).
- **Sign in** with your e-mail and password, or with your mobile number and a code by SMS.
- _Profile and security_: change your password, turn on **two-step verification** (recommended),
  and _sign out everywhere_ if you lost a device.
- _Addresses_: save delivery addresses (region, city or municipality, barangay, street); mark
  one as default.

## 2. Finding products

- Search from the top bar (suggestions appear as you type), or browse **categories** and
  **stores**. Filter and sort by price, newest or rating.
- A product page shows the price (VAT-inclusive), stock, variants, delivery estimate,
  reviews and the store's rating. Add items to your **wishlist** to buy later.
- Approved wholesale customers see wholesale prices and minimum quantities.

## 3. Cart and checkout

1. **Add to cart**. Your cart is kept on the device while signed out and joins your account
   when you sign in. Items are grouped by store: each store ships its own package.
2. **Checkout**: choose the address; for each package choose the delivery option (standard,
   express, same-day where available) with its fee and estimate.
3. **Coupons**: enter a code; the system applies store coupons, marketplace coupons, campaigns
   and free shipping automatically for the best total. **Points**: use loyalty points for part
   of the order.
4. **Payment**: GCash, Maya, card, QR Ph (through PayMongo's secure page — the marketplace never
   sees your card details), bank transfer (upload your proof), or cash on delivery.
5. **Place order**. The total you see is calculated by the server; if a price or stock changed
   meanwhile, checkout shows the new total before anything is charged.

Pay online orders before the deadline shown, or the order is cancelled and the items released.

## 4. Tracking orders

_Orders_ lists every order and package: status (preparing, shipped, delivered), courier and
tracking number, and the history. You get notifications by app, e-mail and SMS (choose which in
_Notifications_). You can cancel a package before it ships, or ask the store to cancel later.

## 5. Returns and refunds

Within the return window after delivery: _Orders › package › Return or refund_, choose the
items and reason, add photos, and ask for a refund or replacement. The store answers; if you
disagree with a refusal, **escalate** and the marketplace decides. Refunds go back to the
original payment method (online payments) or to your bank account (bank transfer and cash on
delivery).

## 6. Reviews

After delivery, _Write a review_ for each item (1–5 stars, headline, text, photos). Reviews
from verified purchases only; one per item. You can edit your review later.

## 7. Rewards, referrals, affiliates

- **Points**: earned on completed orders (shown in _Rewards_), used at checkout, and expire
  after the period stated there.
- **Refer a friend**: share your code; you both get points on their first completed order.
- **Affiliate**: apply in _Affiliate_ to earn commission on sales from your links.

## 8. Help

_Help center_: open a ticket about an order (it goes to the store, and the marketplace can step
in) or a general question; attach photos; follow the conversation there.

## 9. Staying safe

- We never ask for your password, codes or card details by phone, chat or e-mail.
- Pay only through checkout; payments made outside the marketplace are not protected.
- Signing in from a new device? Use _sign out everywhere_ if you do not recognise a session.
